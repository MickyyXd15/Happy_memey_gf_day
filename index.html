To My Beautiful Girlfriend

Happy National Girlfriend's Day, love. I know today is just one date on the calendar, but I wanted to use it as another reason to remind you how important you are to me.

Thank you for being part of my life and for making even the most ordinary days feel special. Some of my favorite memories aren't huge events. They're the simple moments when we're eating together, taking random pictures, laughing about something stupid, or just sitting beside each other.

Good night, baby. 🤍 Before you sleep, gusto ko lang sabihin na thank you for being part of my day. Kahit gaano kapagod o kagulo yung araw, gumagaan talaga pakiramdam ko kapag nakakausap kita. I hope makapagpahinga ka nang maayos tonight. Iwan mo muna lahat ng worries mo at matulog ka nang mahimbing. Tomorrow is another day, and I'll always be here, supporting and loving you every step of the way.
